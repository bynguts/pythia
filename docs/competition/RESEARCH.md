## Problem (pitch hooks)

- **Bantargebang collapse, 8 Mar 2026**: part of the landfill slid after heavy
  rain *while trucks were queuing to unload* — 5 dead, 7 trucks swept away; a
  January 2026 slide had already dragged 3 trucks into a riverbed. Site takes
  ~7,500–7,800 t/day from 1,200+ trucks.
  [GeoEngineer](https://geoengineer.org/news/indonesias-waste-and-mining-slopes-under-pressure-deadly-landfill-collapse-in-bekasi-and-industrial-landslide-in-morowali) ·
  [Asia News Network](https://asianews.network/deadly-landfill-collapse-exposes-jakartas-decades-long-waste-mismanagement/)
- Jakarta: 3.17 Mt in 2024 ≈ 8,688 t/day (SIPSN via [RRI](https://rri.co.id/en/archipelago/2248964/jakarta-targets-rorotan-rdf-to-process-1000-tons-daily));
  other sources 7,354–7,734 t/day — **quote as a range**.
- Bantargebang 50–60 m high, cannot be expanded; open dumping phased out from
  1 Aug 2026. [Kompas](https://lestari.kompas.com/read/2026/02/09/204800086/tpst-bantargebang-tak-bisa-diperluas-lagi-sampah-jakarta-dibawa-ke-mana-) ·
  [Gebrak](https://www.gebrak.id/2026/07/jakarta-hentikan-open-dumping-di-bantargebang-mulai-1-agustus-gunungan-sampah-capai-60-meter.html)
- ~1,700 DLH trucks, mostly Euro II diesel; trucks ≈ 43.2% of Jakarta PM2.5
  (WRI/ITB, Jul 2026). [Katadata](https://katadata.co.id/ekonomi-hijau/energi-baru/6a477167242ba/truk-jadi-biang-polusi-jakarta-wri-dan-itb-usul-transformasi-1700-truk-sampah)
- Flood spikes: Mar 2025 flood → 2,000+ t debris, 5,000 crew on standby.
  [MerahPutih](https://www.merahputih.com/post/amp/pemprov-dki-janji-sampah-pasca-banjir-dibersihkan-sudah-2000-ton-diangkut)
- Indonesia 2024: 34.2 Mt recorded, 40.3% unmanaged (underestimate).
  [Databoks](https://databoks.katadata.co.id/en/environment/statistics/6879e64c06730/40-of-indonesian-waste-unmanaged-in-2024)
- Vietnam: 72,341 t/day domestic waste (2025) [Lao Động](https://news.laodong.vn/moi-truong/viet-nam-phat-sinh-hon-72000-tan-chat-thai-ran-sinh-hoat-moi-ngay-1758066.ldo);
  HCMC ~13,000 t/day, +5.6%/yr; Da Phuoc landfill night-only intake since

  Hanoi Nam Son ~6,400 t/day [Hanoi gov](https://english.hanoi.gov.vn/deparment-district-news/hanoi-promotes-clean-and-beautiful-images-during-tet-2655250205182054969.htm).

## Market and growth

- Smart waste management market: $2.2B (2023) → $8.3B (2032), 15.6% CAGR
  ([Allied](https://prnewswire.co.uk/news-releases/smart-waste-management-market-to-reach-8-3-billion-by-2032-at-15-6-cagr-allied-market-research-302139712.html));
  other publishers 11.9–17.3% CAGR, APAC fastest — **quote as a range**.
- Vietnam smart-city market $7.1B (2024) → $22.7B (2033) ([IMARC](https://www.imarcgroup.com/report/vi/vietnam-smart-cities-market)).
- Policy pull: Indonesia RPJMN targets 100% waste managed by 2029
  ([Setkab](https://setkab.go.id/en/president-prabowo-calls-for-accelerated-national-waste-management-by-2029/));
  Vietnam source-sorting mandatory since 1 Jan 2025 ([VNS](https://vietnamnews.vn/environment/1690041/classifying-solid-waste-at-sources-mandatory-from-january-1.html)).
- **Korea link — K-City Network**: 2026 demonstration grants KRW 300–400M per
  project; 2026 winners include Ho Chi Minh City and Can Tho
  ([MOLIT call](https://smartcity.go.kr/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Calls-for-Projects-2026-K-City-Network-Smart-Solution-Demonstration-ProjectEng.pdf),
  [winners](https://letsdatascience.com/news/korea-announces-winners-of-2026-smart-city-initiative-76e7ae44)).
  Korea's IKN Smart City Cooperation Center (KRW 9.9B ODA) broke ground 18 Jun 2026
  ([Bisnis](https://ekonomi.bisnis.com/read/20260621/45/1982178/ikn-dapat-kucuran-rp11594-miliar-dari-korsel-untuk-proyek-smart-city)).
- Multilateral money: World Bank $350M Local Service Delivery Improvement
  Project for waste in 30 Indonesian local governments (Dec 2025)
  ([World Bank](https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2025/12/18/the-world-bank-approves-a-new-project-to-support-indonesia-achieve-solid-waste-management-targets)).
- Pricing benchmarks: per-truck SaaS ~$50–150/truck/month (blog estimates,
  **no SEA B2G price published**). Illustrative: 1,700 DLH trucks × $50–150 ≈
  $1.0–3.1M ARR — label as an assumption.

## Competitors

| Player | Focus | Gap Pythia fills |
|---|---|---|
| Routeware (ex-Rubicon fleet tech, $94.2M) | US municipal fleet routing | No event/weather volume forecasting; no SEA |
| AMCS (EQT-backed) | ERP + routing for private haulers | Heavy enterprise suite, not B2G command centre |
| Sensoneo, Nordsense, Bigbelly | Bin sensors / smart bins | Hardware capex; doesn't fit TPS/open-pile collection |
| Evreka (Türkiye) | Municipal SaaS + sensors | Closest; no forecasting or SEA focus found |
| Waste4Change, Rekosistem (ID) | Collection/recycling operators | Partners, not DLH supervision tools |
| Veca, mGreen, Grac (VN) | Citizen recycling apps | Citizen side only |

No competitor found combining district-level weather/event-driven volume
forecasting, live route supervision and landfill-queue management for
developing-city governments (our synthesis).

## Evidence that the approach pays

- Route optimisation, peer-reviewed: −10.8% fuel / −14.2% time (Ghana,
  [UG](https://pure.ug.edu.gh/en/publications/effect-of-optimal-routing-on-travel-distance-travel-time-and-fuel/));
  −14.3% fuel (Al Ain, [UAEU](https://research.uaeu.ac.ae/en/publications/optimization-of-fuel-consumption-for-municipal-solid-waste-collec/)).
  Plan around **5–15% fuel**.
- Digital twins in city ops: Virtual Singapore, Seoul S-Map (605 km² 3D twin),
  Naples €7.2M waste-collection twin
  ([Kurrant](https://kurrant.com/kurrantly-news/naples-backs-ai-powered-waste-digital-twin-with-e7-2m-state-approved-research-plan/)).
- AR for waste crews: **no deployment found** — Pythia AR is novel, not proven.
