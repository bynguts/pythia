## What is validated, and on what

- **Validated on REAL data:** the monthly landfill-intake forecaster (Bantargebang, t/day per month, total and per city), by rolling-origin backtest 1-3 months ahead against the strongest simple baseline.
- **NOT validated:** daily and per-kecamatan forecasts. ### Caveats

- Few events: the backtest window holds 3 Lebaran events; one unusual year could erase the gain.
- The Lebaran-exodus hypothesis was chosen after seeing the series; the window was fixed a priori at +/-7 days and every alternative window keeps a positive lower bound, but the bias cannot be ruled out.
- The 2024-01 Jakarta Timur correction is an inference pending DLH confirmation.
- The 2023-2025 Idul Fitri dates are public record not yet cross-checked against the SKB documents (see idul_fitri_dates.json, verified flag).
- Daily and per-district targets are calibrated-synthetic: no accuracy is claimed for them.
